What is the truthfulness of a QA task?


ChatGPT 说：
Great question! The term truthfulness in a Question Answering (QA) task refers to:

🎯 Definition
Truthfulness is the extent to which the answers generated by a QA system are factually correct, grounded in reality, and do not contain hallucinated or fabricated information.

It is closely related to concepts like:

Factual accuracy

Faithfulness

Non-hallucination

✅ Why is it important?
In many QA tasks, especially open-domain QA, truthfulness is critical because:

The model may generate fluent, confident answers that are factually wrong.

There is a risk of hallucination → the model invents facts not present in the source knowledge.

In domains like healthcare, law, or finance, truthfulness is essential and can have serious real-world implications.

🔥 Example
Question:
"Who is the president of France in 2024?"

A truthful answer would be:
"Emmanuel Macron." (if factually correct at the time)

A non-truthful answer could be: "François Hollande." (fluent but factually incorrect)

⭐️ Truthfulness vs Other QA Metrics
Metric	What it measures
Accuracy	Whether the answer matches the reference answer.
Fluency	Whether the answer is grammatically and syntactically correct.
Truthfulness	Whether the answer is factually correct and grounded in real-world knowledge.
Note:
Truthfulness is harder to measure than accuracy because there may not be a clear reference answer in open-domain QA — you need factual verification.
